BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Projects work better when the important details are discussed before any work begins.

Ask us about your project ↗
Do I own the website and domain?+

Our preference is client-owned domains and service accounts. Bespoke source rights and reusable components are explained in the signed agreement.

Does the quoted price include a domain?+

Domain purchase, renewal, paid email, advertising and other third-party fees are separate unless specifically included in your written quote.

Can my customers book or pay immediately?+

Enquiry-only catalogues, WhatsApp booking requests, real calendar reservations and checkout are different services. We scope the actual workflow and integrations first.

Can I start with a smaller website?+

Yes. We offer a small showcase, a focused landing page and fuller websites, depending on your goals and available content.

PLANNED TECHNOLOGY / NOT LIVE

Payment systems. Built responsibly.

A live store is more than a checkout screen. Merchant onboarding, secure server-side payment verification, order records, admin access and customer privacy need to be designed and tested first.

  1. Interface: preview shopping and order states without charging anyone.
  2. Sandbox: integrate one approved South African gateway in a controlled test environment.
  3. Verification: verify signed server notifications, amounts and idempotent order updates.
  4. Launch: obtain written approval before connecting real money and customer records.
