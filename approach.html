COMMON QUESTIONS

The details,
made simple.

Do I need to know anything about domains or hosting?

No. We'll explain what's needed in plain language, help you set up accounts in your name and handle the agreed technical configuration. Domain renewals and any paid provider fees are separate.

Can you promise I'll rank first on Google?

No agency can responsibly guarantee a specific search ranking. We can create a sound foundation through site structure, useful copy and local search optimisation, then measure and improve results.

Can customers book appointments through the site?

Yes, with the right scope. A simple request flow lets a customer suggest a slot for staff to confirm. A real-time scheduling system is a separate service with live availability and conflict prevention.

Who owns the website when it's finished?

The agreement defines custom deliverables and rights, including ownership of approved deliverables on full payment. Clients should control their own domain and service accounts, while Jackman may retain rights to reusable tools/components.

PLANNED TECHNOLOGY / NOT LIVE

Payment systems. Built responsibly.

A live store is more than a checkout screen. Merchant onboarding, secure server-side payment verification, order records, admin access and customer privacy need to be designed and tested first.

  1. Interface: preview shopping and order states without charging anyone.
  2. Sandbox: integrate one approved South African gateway in a controlled test environment.
  3. Verification: verify signed server notifications, amounts and idempotent order updates.
  4. Launch: obtain written approval before connecting real money and customer records.
