COMMON QUESTIONS

What happens
after checkout?

We scope the features you can actually operate rather than pretending a static demo is a live store.

Does this include a live payment gateway?+

A scoped ecommerce build includes integration of one approved hosted payment provider. Your own merchant onboarding and final payment testing are required; provider fees are separate.

Can customers track an order?+

Yes, if your approved build includes a real status and order-data workflow. Customer order status and courier tracking are additional features that are assessed and quoted separately.

Will a customer get automatic courier updates?+

Not by default. A courier integration, SMS or email notices, status sync and third-party accounts require further technical scoping and consent to any provider charges.

Can I start with an enquiry-only catalogue?+

Yes. An enquiry-only catalogue is a different, lower-complexity service and does not accept verified orders or real payments. Compare the separate catalogue pricing.

PLANNED TECHNOLOGY / NOT LIVE

Payment systems. Built responsibly.

A live store is more than a checkout screen. Merchant onboarding, secure server-side payment verification, order records, admin access and customer privacy need to be designed and tested first.

  1. Interface: preview shopping and order states without charging anyone.
  2. Sandbox: integrate one approved South African gateway in a controlled test environment.
  3. Verification: verify signed server notifications, amounts and idempotent order updates.
  4. Launch: obtain written approval before connecting real money and customer records.
